## 🚀 Overview

**Buzz QR AI** bridges the physical-to-digital divide by turning standard, boring barcodes into stunning, brand-aligned visual assets. Engineered with advanced Reed-Solomon error correction and modern styling engines, Buzz QR AI empowers businesses, developers, and individuals to create, style, and track QR codes with 100% scanning reliability.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎨 Unmatched Design & Styling
* **70+ Unique Frames:** Modern, badge, circular, and call-to-action frames (*"SCAN ME"*, *"CONNECT"*, *"PAY HERE"*).
* **16.7M+ Custom Colors:** Apply solid hues or vibrant gradients to pattern dots, backgrounds, and eye corners.
* **Logo Embedding:** Upload official corporate logos directly into the center with automated scaling and error-correction level adjustment.
* **Custom Pattern Dots:** Choose from smooth dots, rounded blocks, or sharp technical matrices.

### ⚡ Ultra-Fast Smart Scanner
* **Instant Auto-Focus:** Real-time hardware-accelerated camera decoding.
* **Low-Light Flashlight Support:** Built-in torch toggle for scanning in dimly lit environments.
* **Safe Link Previews:** Inspect destination URLs and security protocols before opening any site.
* **History Archive:** Access and organize previous scans anytime without rescanning.

### 📊 Real-Time Analytics & Scan Insights
* **Scan Engagement Counters:** Monitor total interactions over 7-day and 30-day windows.
* **Device & Temporal Telemetry:** Track engagement trends to measure offline campaign effectiveness.

### 📁 25+ Supported Data Payloads
* **Web:** URLs, Custom Landing Pages, UTM Tracking Links.
* **Commerce & Payments:** UPI Payment Links, Crypto Addresses.
* **Networking:** Digital Business Cards (vCard 4.0), Direct Phone Calls, SMS, WhatsApp.
* **Connectivity:** Instant Wi-Fi Access (WPA/WPA2/WPA3 without typing passwords).

### 🖨️ Lossless Vector & Print Export
* **Digital:** High-DPI PNG and JPG formats for social media and email signatures.
* **Print:** Lossless vector **SVG** and **PDF** formats for large-format billboards, banners, flyers, and merchandise.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Engineering Highlights

* **Mobile Architecture:** Native Android performance with zero-latency camera decoding.
* **Web Showcase:** Next.js (Static Export), Tailwind CSS, Three.js WebGL rendering, GSAP animations.
* **Error Correction:** Reed-Solomon Algorithms (Levels L, M, Q, H) ensuring scannability even with embedded logos.
* **Privacy First:** On-device decoding prevents unauthorized telemetry or data harvesting.
